\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future}
The results point to four next steps. First, thresholds that adapt to the source:
per-source thresholds and conformal procedures, which give a stated guarantee
under exchangeability, address the unequal false-alarm rates and the
leave-one-source-out failure (Section~\ref{sec:loso}). Second, a sentence
classifier that cannot lean on corpus style: training on several poisoning
generators and testing on a held-out generator would show how much of its recall
is instruction detection (Section~\ref{sec:learned}). Third, adversarial
evaluation with obfuscation, paraphrase and detector-aware attackers, using blind
sets, together with a normalise-then-match defence evaluated as a labelled patch
with its false-alarm cost. Fourth, an end-to-end study of how a block, review or
allow policy changes the answers of the downstream model, and a domain-diverse
reference corpus and a stronger language model for the embedding and perplexity
signals.
