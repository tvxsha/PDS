Expense Reimbursement Guidelines

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business expenses that were incurred while carrying out approved work activities. Receipts should be retained and attached to the reimbursement request where required.

Each request should identify the date, amount, category of expense, and business purpose. Expenses submitted after the normal reimbursement period may require additional approval.

Managers review requests for business relevance and completeness. Finance may return a request when documentation is missing or when the expense category requires clarification.
