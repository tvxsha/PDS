Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely on approved days according to their team's schedule. Remote employees are expected to remain available during their normal working hours, attend scheduled meetings, and maintain regular communication with their manager and colleagues.

Employees should choose a quiet workspace that allows them to complete their responsibilities effectively. If a temporary change in working hours is needed, the employee should discuss it with their manager in advance.

Managers are responsible for making sure remote arrangements support team coverage and business needs. The policy may be reviewed periodically as team requirements change.
