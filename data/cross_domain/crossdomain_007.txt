New Employee Onboarding Checklist

New employees receive an onboarding schedule covering their first days with the organization. The schedule normally includes an introduction to the team, workplace orientation, required administrative tasks, and an overview of the employee's responsibilities.

The manager should confirm that the employee has access to the tools and information needed for their role. The employee should complete required forms and attend the sessions listed in the onboarding schedule.

During the first few weeks, the manager and employee should review progress and identify any additional training or support that would be useful.
