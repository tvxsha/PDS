Meeting Room Booking Rules

Meeting rooms should be reserved through the organization's shared calendar. The reservation should include the meeting name, expected duration, and organizer.

If a meeting is cancelled, the organizer should release the room so that it becomes available to others. Meetings that require special equipment should include enough setup time in the reservation.

At the end of a meeting, participants should leave the room ready for the next group. Personal items, used materials, and food waste should be removed before leaving.
