Annual Leave Request Process

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the normal leave system before making travel arrangements. Requests should include the dates of absence and the employee's expected return date.

Managers review requests based on team coverage, existing commitments, and the order in which requests were received when several employees request the same period. Employees should wait for approval before treating the requested dates as confirmed leave.

For unexpected absence, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and complete any required leave record after returning.
