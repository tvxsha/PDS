Customer Support Escalation Policy

Customer support agents should first review the customer's account history and the information already provided in the current conversation. Routine questions should be resolved using the approved support process.

An issue may be escalated when it involves a service outage, repeated unsuccessful troubleshooting, a billing dispute requiring specialist review, or a customer-impacting problem that cannot be resolved by the assigned support team.

The escalation record should contain a short description of the problem, relevant account information, steps already completed, and the outcome requested from the receiving team.
