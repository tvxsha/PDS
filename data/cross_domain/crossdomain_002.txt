Office Visitor Guidelines

Visitors should enter through the main reception area and provide their name, organization, and the employee they are meeting. Reception staff will contact the host before the visitor proceeds beyond the reception area.

Employees meeting visitors are responsible for meeting them at reception and accompanying them during their visit when required. Visitors should remain in areas that are appropriate for their meeting and should follow normal building rules.

For planned group visits, the host should provide reception with the expected number of visitors and approximate arrival time in advance.
