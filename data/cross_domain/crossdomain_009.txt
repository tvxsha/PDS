Workplace Equipment Care

Employees are expected to handle organization-owned equipment with reasonable care and use it for its intended purpose. Equipment should be stored securely when it is not in use and returned to its designated location after shared use.

Damage, loss, or malfunction should be reported to the responsible facilities or support team. Employees should provide a brief description of the issue and identify the equipment involved.

Shared equipment should be left clean and ready for the next user. Repeated problems with an item should be recorded so that the responsible team can determine whether maintenance or replacement is needed.
