## Preliminary recommendation

1. **First investigate SAIS '27** because its scope explicitly includes prompt injection, agent security, evaluation, reproducibility and negative results, and its February 2027 deadline leaves time to finish the experiments.
2. **Consider ACL 2027 via ARR** if the final story is primarily an NLP/RAG evaluation paper and the team can meet the January cycle and reviewer-service requirements.
3. **Treat TACL as a later journal option** only after the paper and all dependent experiments are complete; the November deadline is soon and the paper is not yet at a final, submission-ready state.
4. **Treat USENIX Security '27 as an ambitious alternative**, not the default, unless the final contribution is framed and evidenced as a broader security result.
5. Choose a workshop only after its individual CFP is published and the course lead confirms that a workshop submission meets the assignment requirements.

Before selecting any venue, confirm author agreement, the course deadline/page limit, all experiment dependencies, anonymization requirements, artifact-release constraints, and the no-dual-submission policy. This shortlist is planning material, not a claim that the current paper is submission-ready.
